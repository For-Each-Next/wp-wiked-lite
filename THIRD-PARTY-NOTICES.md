## Wikimedia Codex icons

`cdxIconUndo` from `@wikimedia/codex-icons` version 2.7.0. These SVG path assets
retain their upstream MIT license. Their complete notice is included in both
`dist/wiked_lite.min.js` and `dist/wiked_lite.user.js`. The icon package and the
Vue/Codex component runtime are not bundled into the browser program.

Both artifact headings identify the MIT source and link to the
[upstream Codex repository](https://gerrit.wikimedia.org/g/design/codex/).
